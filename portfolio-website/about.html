About Me

I am a final-year B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham with strong foundations in Java, JavaScript, MERN Stack, REST APIs, MongoDB, and Data Structures & Algorithms. I am passionate about software development, backend engineering, and building scalable full-stack applications with clean and efficient system design. I continuously improve my problem-solving skills through real-world projects and competitive programming.

I have worked on projects involving AI, machine learning, computer vision, optimization algorithms, and secure software systems, including an AI-powered dental diagnosis platform, EV route optimization system, OCR using Hidden Markov Models, underwater garbage detection using YOLO, and post-quantum cryptography-based audio encryption. I was also selected for Amazon ML Summer School and have contributed to research-oriented work with a paper accepted at ICIRSET 2025.
